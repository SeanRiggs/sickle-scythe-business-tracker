# Check the v5.6 workspace

Built from deployed v5.5 at `974a86b3b0b542203b3614466aefc62560c66e37`. This release changes presentation and task navigation. It does not migrate records or change accounting calculations, stock movement rules, Microsoft authentication, or OneDrive storage keys.

## Find your everyday work

- **New sale:** enter customer/date, product/quantity/price, and optional payment. Review the total, then Save sale.
- **Record payment:** find a customer or invoice, select the correct invoice, and enter the money actually received. The picker starts with outstanding balances. Include paid invoices and credits when recording an additional payment. No invoice is selected automatically, and nothing is saved until Save Payment.
- **New purchase order:** enter supplier/dates, product lines, then shipping/fees/warranty credit. Review the order total. Saving creates the order; Receive stock adds inventory later.
- **Quick actions:** the same shortcuts are available in the header from every section. The plus icon is the compact phone version.
- **Find payment / receipt:** searches recorded payments, including records needing correction or a printable receipt.

Navigation labels are simpler: Products is **Inventory**, Receivables is **Balances & credits**, Data Health is **Record checks**, and History is **Activity**. The underlying records are unchanged.

## Automated checks

```sh
node --check tracker-v56.js
node --check service-worker.js
node --test tests/*.test.cjs
python3 scripts/check_public_files.py
python3 scripts/package_release.py
```

The browser workflow uses fictional records and mocked Microsoft services. Chromium and WebKit cover desktop, phone, small portrait and landscape. It checks payment lookup (including paid/credited invoices), draft retention across task switching and rotation, search contrast/focus, linked purchase credits, returns, replacements, exact record warnings, offline caching and reconnect behavior. A browser engine is not a physical iPhone test.

## Review before everyday use

1. Refresh the app and confirm **v5.6**. Keep the normal backup routine and use one editing device at a time.
2. Check the three dashboard shortcuts and Quick actions from another section.
3. Open Record payment, search for an invoice, and verify the customer, invoice ID and balance. Cancel to leave records unchanged.
4. Check the actual device in portrait and landscape. Search should be easy to read; actions, amount badges and form labels should align without horizontal scrolling.
5. Start an unsaved sale or purchase draft. Switch sections and back: the draft should remain. Closing/reloading the tab still discards unsaved drafts.
6. When making a real transaction, review the amount and selected invoice before saving, then wait for **Saved with OneDrive**.

Do not create fictional sales in a production database for testing. All screenshots, test fixtures and public artifacts must use fictional records; never upload customer screenshots or private exports to the repository.
