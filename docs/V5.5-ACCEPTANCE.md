## Employee walkthrough

1. Receive a purchase in two deliveries with actual dates. Product history shows both, plus any earlier undated balance, and its sum matches on-hand stock.
2. Record a two-unit sale, then a one-unit resellable return. History opens the exact invoice and preserves genuine payments.
3. Create a warranty and change approval. Stock stays unchanged. Send one replacement from inventory; stock falls by one and the confirmed cost appears in Reports. Send another directly from the supplier; local stock stays unchanged.
4. Link replacement shipping to a previously saved expense. It is deducted once, not twice. Try to reduce that expense below linked cost or void it: saving must be blocked.
5. Reverse an incorrectly entered replacement with a reason. Stock and separately recorded cost reverse; the original event and any actual linked expense remain.
6. Record lost stock with a confirmed cost. Both inventory and estimated profit decline. Reverse an incorrect adjustment and check its audit.
7. Add fictional older ambiguous warranty/deduction records. Profit becomes provisional. Review their total cost, leaving unknown dates blank, and verify stock does not change. Monthly reports exclude undated separate costs; All Time includes them.
8. Open two similar warnings. Each must identify its exact record. An ambiguous warranty warning requires an explicit match and reason, never choosing the first imported claim. A quantity warning edits quantity; a purchase-date correction preserves ETA and receipt dates.
9. Reload, rotate portrait/landscape and inspect large text. Offline changes survive reload; reconnect uploads only after the existing conflict checks. Review all retained settings and unknown fields.

Neither failed attempt may change saved records. Clear an older purchase date and confirm it stays unknown in details, review and the editor while the original historical field remains preserved.
